## How to Choose an SEO or Web Design Company

Before signing a contract, compare providers using a few practical
criteria:

-   **Portfolio:** Look for recent projects that resemble your industry,
    audience, and goals.
-   **SEO approach:** Ask about technical SEO, keyword research, local
    landing pages, content, Google Business Profile, and reporting.
-   **Website performance:** Check mobile usability, accessibility,
    loading speed, and conversion paths.
-   **Transparency:** Request a written scope of work, deliverables,
    timeline, pricing, and reporting schedule.
-   **Ownership and access:** Confirm that you retain access to your
    domain, website, analytics, Google Business Profile, and content.
-   **Evidence of results:** Ask for relevant case studies and clarify
    how the agency measures leads, sales, and organic visibility.
-   **Communication:** Choose a team that explains its process clearly
    and sets realistic expectations. No agency can guarantee a specific
    Google ranking.
