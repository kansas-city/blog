## How to Choose a Web Design Company

-   **Portfolio:** Look for recent work that matches your industry,
    brand, and project complexity.
-   **Mobile experience:** Make sure the design works well on phones,
    tablets, and desktops.
-   **Performance and accessibility:** Ask how the team approaches page
    speed, accessibility, security, and usability.
-   **SEO foundations:** Confirm that the site will have sensible page
    structure, editable metadata, redirects when needed, and analytics
    setup.
-   **Content and conversion:** Make sure the site clearly explains your
    services and makes it easy for visitors to call, request a quote, or
    buy.
-   **Ownership:** Confirm that you will control your domain, hosting,
    content, website files, and analytics accounts.
-   **Support and costs:** Get the scope, timeline, revision limits,
    maintenance, and any recurring fees in writing.
