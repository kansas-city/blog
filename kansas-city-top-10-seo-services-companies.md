## What to Compare Before Hiring an SEO Company

Use these questions when reviewing proposals:

-   **Goals:** Will the agency focus on calls, qualified leads, sales,
    or another measurable business outcome?
-   **Local SEO:** Does it review your Google Business Profile, local
    landing pages, citations, and location-specific search terms?
-   **Technical SEO:** Does the work cover crawling, indexing, site
    speed, mobile usability, and structured data where appropriate?
-   **Content quality:** Will content answer real customer questions and
    reflect your expertise, rather than simply repeat keywords?
-   **Reporting:** Will you receive clear reports on work completed,
    organic visibility, website traffic, and leads?
-   **Pricing and terms:** Are deliverables, additional fees, ownership,
    cancellation terms, and timelines written down?
-   **Realistic expectations:** SEO takes time, and no agency can
    honestly guarantee a specific Google ranking.
